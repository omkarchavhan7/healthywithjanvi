Explore Our Laddu Benefits

Ragi Laddu

Wholesome finger millet with traditional flavour.

Benefits of Ragi Laddu

  • Ragi is naturally rich in calcium.
  • Provides dietary fibre.
  • Contains carbohydrates for energy.
  • Provides minerals, including iron.

Nutritional value depends on the recipe and serving size.

Moong Dal Laddu

A classic sweet made with gram flour.

Benefits of Moong dal Laddu

  • Good source of protein.
  • Provides energy.
  • Contains dietary fiber.
  • Provides essential nutrients

Nutritional value depends on the recipe and serving size.

Dry Fruit Laddu

A delicious mix of nuts and other wholesome ingredients.

Benefits of Dry Fruit Laddu

  • Nuts provide unsaturated fats.
  • Many nuts contain plant-based protein.
  • Provides energy and a variety of minerals.
  • Ingredients such as dates add natural sweetness.

Benefits vary with the nuts, seeds and sweeteners used.

Coconut Laddu

A sweet treat with the rich flavour of coconut.

Benefits of Coconut Laddoo

  • Coconut provides dietary fibre.
  • Contains fats that contribute energy.
  • Provides some minerals.
  • A tasty option for coconut lovers.

Added sugar and other ingredients affect the final nutrition.
